When a personal data breach occurs, the organisation must assess the risk to individuals. If the breach is likely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms, the supervisory authority must be notified without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. If the breach is likely to result in a high risk, the affected individuals must also be informed directly and in clear language. Every breach, even one that is not reported, should be recorded in an internal register with the facts, its effects and the remedial action taken. A processor that discovers a breach must inform the controller without undue delay.

A data protection impact assessment, or DPIA, is required before processing that is likely to result in a high risk to individuals. Typical triggers include large-scale monitoring of publicly accessible areas, systematic profiling with significant effects, and large-scale processing of sensitive categories of data. A DPIA describes the processing, assesses its necessity and proportionality, evaluates the risks and lists the measures planned to address them. Some organisations must also appoint a data protection officer. This applies to public authorities and to organisations whose core activities involve regular, large-scale monitoring or large-scale processing of sensitive data. The officer advises on compliance and acts as a contact point for the supervisory authority.

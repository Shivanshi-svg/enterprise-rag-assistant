Individuals have a number of rights over their personal data. The right of access lets a person obtain a copy of the data an organisation holds about them. The right to rectification allows them to correct inaccurate records. The right to erasure, sometimes called the right to be forgotten, lets them request deletion in certain circumstances. They can also request restriction of processing, and they have a right to data portability, which means receiving their data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. People may object to processing based on legitimate interests or used for direct marketing. Organisations normally have to respond to such requests without undue delay and within one month.

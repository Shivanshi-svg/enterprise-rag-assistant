Personal data may only leave the European Economic Area under specific conditions. The simplest route is an adequacy decision, where the European Commission has determined that a country offers an essentially equivalent level of protection. Without an adequacy decision, organisations can rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or binding corporate rules. When using standard contractual clauses, the exporter should assess whether the laws of the destination country undermine the protection and add supplementary measures, such as strong encryption, where needed. As a last resort, narrow derogations such as explicit consent for a specific transfer may apply.

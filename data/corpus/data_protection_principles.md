Organisations that process personal data in the EU follow a set of core principles. Data must be processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, so people are told what happens to their information. Purpose limitation means data is collected for specified, explicit reasons and is not reused in incompatible ways. Data minimisation requires that only the data needed for the stated purpose is collected. Accuracy obliges organisations to keep records correct and up to date. Storage limitation means personal data is kept only as long as necessary. Integrity and confidentiality require appropriate security, such as encryption and access control. Finally, accountability means the organisation must be able to demonstrate compliance with all of these principles.
